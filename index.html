Welcome to campus

A place for faith, friendship and service.

A welcoming Christian student community at Kyambogo University in Kampala. This site is an introduction and a starting point for students looking to connect with Christian fellowship on campus.

Growing in Christ and serving one another on campus.

— A simple invitation

Kyambogo University is a diverse public university on Kyambogo Hill. Its published student information recognises several places of worship on campus, including St. Kakumba Chapel, Uganda Martyrs Chaplaincy and the SDA Chapel.

The public sources we found do not confirm the Union's current meeting times, executive, chapter list or official contact channels. Please check with the University's student support or chaplaincy contacts before travelling to a gathering.

Fellowship at Kyambogo

Looking for Christian fellowship at Kyambogo? The University lists several places of worship, but its published pages do not confirm a KYU-CU chapter directory or weekly programme. Contact the student support office to confirm the current Union meeting point and schedule.

St. Kakumba Chapel

  • The University's student affairs information lists St. Kakumba Chapel as an Anglican place of worship on campus.
  • Sunday service times published by the Academic Registrar are 8:00 a.m., 9:30 a.m. and 10:00 a.m. These are chapel services, not a confirmed KYU-CU meeting schedule. Please verify current times before attending.

Uganda Martyrs Chaplaincy

  • The University's student affairs page identifies the Catholic chaplaincy opposite Kulubya Hall. It lists Sunday Mass and weekday evening Mass; check the University's current information for details.

SDA Chapel

  • The University lists an SDA Chapel for Saturday prayers. Contact the University to confirm the current location and schedule.

Campus fellowship

  • For the current KYU-CU gathering, Bible study groups, or student-led activities, ask the University student support or chaplaincy office. We do not publish unverified chapter names or times.

Our story

Rooted in the Kyambogo campus community

A growing university, a campus community

Kyambogo Christian Union is a student fellowship associated with Kyambogo University. Publicly accessible records about the Union's own founding and history are limited, so we do not publish an unverified origin story or date.

Kyambogo University was established in 2003 through the merger of three institutions. The University's official student information identifies several places of worship serving its campus community, including St. Kakumba Chapel.

If you have verified historical material, photographs or alumni records about the Christian Union, we would be glad to include them with attribution.
